About the Diamond Bib Cock

The Diamond Bib Cock from PLUNGE is engineered to deliver an unparalleled blend of form and function. Crafted from premium-grade brass, this bib cock is built to withstand the rigors of daily use while maintaining its stunning aesthetic appeal for years to come.

Its multi-layer chrome plating process ensures a mirror-like finish that resists tarnishing, corrosion, and scratches. The high-quality ceramic cartridge inside provides smooth, precise water flow control with a quarter-turn operation, eliminating drips and ensuring water conservation.

Whether you're outfitting a modern bathroom or upgrading your kitchen, the Diamond Bib Cock brings a refined, contemporary look that complements any decor. Its ergonomic handle design offers comfortable grip and effortless operation for users of all ages.
